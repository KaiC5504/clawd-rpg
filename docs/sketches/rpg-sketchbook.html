The ten original sketches

Ideas that fit any of them

Not animated here. These are mechanics that could sit on top of whichever game you pick.

  • HP is your context windowIt drains as the conversation grows. A compact is a night at the inn, and HP comes back.
  • MP is the 5-hour limitBig spells (subagents, long commands) cost more. Running dry makes Clawd slow and tired, like today's exhausted pose.
  • Subagents join the partyEach Agent call drops in a party member with a class from its job: Explore is a scout, Plan a mage, general-purpose a knight.
  • Enemies come from real errorsTypeError beetles, flaky-test ghosts, lint slimes, a merge-conflict hydra. A bestiary fills up across every repo.
  • Loot is named after real workBlade of scenes.ts, Hydra Scale from a merge conflict you solved. Rarity from test counts or diff size.
  • The CI race becomes a raidYour best run is a rival party's ghost, and the build steps are dungeon floors.
  • Rotate or chooseSeveral games can take turns like today's game cabinet, or you pin one per repo.
  • Clicking the bandIf Claude Code passes mouse clicks to the band, a click could parry or grab loot. To check in the API before we promise it.
